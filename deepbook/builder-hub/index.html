Spot

What it is

Sui's canonical onchain order book. Real order-book depth, not synthetic pricing.

How it works

Liquidity is shared across all DeepBook markets. No isolated vaults, no bootstrapping. Every order settles atomically in a single Sui transaction.

What makes it special

The deepest onchain order book in DeFi.
Every integration taps the same liquidity pool, so depth compounds rather than fragments as the ecosystem grows.

Margin

What it is

Onchain margin built directly into the DeepBook order book. Borrow, trade, and settle within a single composable framework.

How it works

Borrowers post collateral and draw from DeepBook's shared liquidity pool. Positions open, execute, and close atomically; no offchain sequencer, no separate margin engine.

What makes it special

Margin that composes with everything else in the stack. Pair it with Spot for leveraged order-book trading. Pair it with Predictions for leveraged binary positions. Integrate via SDK or API. Flexible enough to fit your architecture.

Predict

What it is

Binary options that resolve a simple question: will an asset be above or below a given price by a specific date?

How it works

Powered by Block Scholes options pricing with continuous strikes at any price point. Settlement is automatic and fully onchain; no off-chain resolution, no trusted intermediary.

What makes it special

Native leverage through Margin. Combine with Spot for instant settlement. Compose across assets and expiries into products no single protocol can offer.
