Start BuildingStart Trading

Spot

Trade against Sui's deepest order book. Real-time prices, tight spreads, and shared liquidity across every app in the ecosystem.

Margin

Leverage your positions on the same order book. Shared collateral means more depth and better liquidation prices than siloed platforms.

Predict

Trade on whether an asset will be above or below a given price by a specific date. A new category of trading, native to Sui.

On most platforms, each trading type is its own silo. On Sui, they work together. Apps can combine spot, margin, and predictions into strategies and instruments you can't access anywhere else. That's what 'Only on Sui' actually means for you as a trader.

Why traders choose Sui

01

Deeper markets, better prices

Every app draws from the same shared liquidity. More depth means tighter spreads and less slippage.

02

Transparent execution

An open order book where you see exactly where buyers and sellers are. No hidden spreads, no black-box pricing.

03

Your keys, your funds

Trade directly from your wallet. No deposits to a centralized exchange. Your assets stay under your control.

04

Speed that matters

Sui finalizes transactions in under a second. Place, modify, or cancel orders with the responsiveness of a professional trading platform.
